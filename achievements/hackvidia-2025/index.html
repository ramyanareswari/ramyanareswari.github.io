Overview

Hackvidia is the AI hackathon track of Arkavidia, one of the larger student technology events in Indonesia. Team rawrXD reached the finals and placed 4th.

We built Street Tweets, an AI system that turns social media posts about road damage into traceable, prioritised reports for the authorities responsible for fixing them.

The judging covered more than the model: problem discovery, solution design, business viability, and a final pitch to industry judges.

Features

  • Problem discovery — Identifying why road damage reporting fails in practice, not just that it does.
  • Solution design — Designing a pipeline across NLP, computer vision and classification.
  • Business viability — Arguing who pays, who benefits and why the system would be adopted.
  • Pitching — Presenting the solution to industry judges in the final round.
