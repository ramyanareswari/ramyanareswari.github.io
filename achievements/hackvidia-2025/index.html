4th Place, Hackvidia 2025

Arkavidia 9.0, ITB · National

Context

Competed in Hackvidia, the AI hackathon track of Arkavidia 9.0 hosted by ITB. Team rawrXD reached the final round and placed 4th.

The project was Street Tweets, an AI system for road damage reporting built over the course of the competition.

The output

  • An AI system that turns social media posts about road damage into reports for the authorities responsible for fixing them.
  • Location resolution — NLP reads the post text to identify where the damage is.
  • Damage assessment — computer vision grades severity from the photo.
  • Priority classification — a classification model ranks reports by repair priority.
  • Report tracking — each report carries an ID through validation, routing to the responsible authority, and completion.
  • Public dashboard — report volume, status distribution and reports by province.

My role

  • Solution design — designing the pipeline across NLP, computer vision and classification.
  • Business viability — the business case presented as part of the pitch.
  • Pitching — presenting to industry judges in the final round.
