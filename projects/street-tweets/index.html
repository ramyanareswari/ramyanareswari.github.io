Street Tweets

An AI system that turns social media posts about road damage into reports for the authorities responsible for fixing them.

Overview

People report road damage on social media rather than through official channels, where reporting means knowing which agency to contact and waiting on manual verification.

Street Tweets takes those posts as input. NLP resolves the location from the post text, computer vision assesses damage severity from the photo, and a classification model assigns a repair priority.

Each report carries an ID that can be tracked through validation, routing to the responsible authority, and completion. A public dashboard shows report volume, status distribution and reports by province.

Features

  • Tweet Your Street — Post a location and a photo. The system handles extraction, severity and priority.
  • Location resolution — NLP reads the post text to identify where the damage is.
  • Damage assessment — Computer vision grades severity from the photo.
  • Priority classification — A classification model ranks reports by repair priority.
  • Track Your Report — A report ID moves through received, validation, sent to authority, in progress and completed.
  • Be Informed — A public dashboard showing report volume, status distribution and reports by province.
