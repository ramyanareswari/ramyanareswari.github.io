Overview

My day job, and the reason the rest of this portfolio is credible: I am already delivering enterprise technology in a professional environment, for financial institutions, under real constraints.

The work spans requirements, stakeholder alignment, UAT and implementation support, carrying scope from discovery through to implementation rather than handing it over at the spec.

Features

  • Discovery — Understanding what the business actually needs before anything is written down.
  • Requirements — Turning that into structured requirements engineers can build against.
  • Stakeholder alignment — Keeping business and technical stakeholders pointed at the same outcome.
  • UAT — Validating that what was built matches what was asked for.
  • Implementation support — Staying with the solution through rollout.

Notes

The pattern underneath all of it is translation: business problem in, buildable specification out, working solution back.
